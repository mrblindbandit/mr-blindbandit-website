Independent artist release guide

A start-to-finish framework for releasing music without losing control of the details.

Define the releaseConfirm rightsDeliver carefullyMaintain the catalog
01

Define the release

Choose the approved recording, artist presentation, title, version, audience and purpose before building the campaign.

02

Confirm rights

Document master ownership, writer shares, producer terms, samples, features and artwork permission before distribution.

03

Deliver carefully

Use consistent metadata and identifiers, review store dates and preserve the distributor confirmation reference.

04

Maintain the catalog

After launch, archive masters and artwork, verify links, record corrections and keep credits current on the official website.
