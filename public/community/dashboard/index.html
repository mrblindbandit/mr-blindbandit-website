Your place in the Blindbandit community

This dashboard summarizes your community participation: profile basics, recent activity, and shortcuts into Blindbandit Mobile, the fan hub, and optional mailing-list preferences.

Interactive widgets load after JavaScript confirms your Clerk session. The guidance below explains what you can do even when dynamic panels are still loading.

  • Profile. Keep your display name accurate and appropriate for a public music community.
  • Activity. Review recent posts, follows, or messages from Blindbandit Mobile when linked to your account.
  • Listening. Jump to the catalog, discover guide, or verified artist mobile profile.
  • Support the artist. Optional support links live on the public Support page (PayPal / Wise) — never requested via DM.
  • Safety. Report harassment through in-product tools or safety@mrblindbandit.net.
