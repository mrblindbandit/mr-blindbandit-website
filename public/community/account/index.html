COMMUNITY · ACCOUNT

Your Blindbandit community account

Sign in securely with your production Clerk account, choose a public display name, and join the conversation around Mr. Blindbandit’s music and Blindbandit Records.

This account surface is for fans and creators who want to participate in community features. It is separate from the private Label OS portal used by invitation-only operators. Clerk manages authentication, Google sign-in, multi-factor options, sessions, and recovery.

  • Create or sign in. Use Clerk with Google or email. Keep recovery codes private.
  • Display name. Pick a name you are comfortable showing publicly beside comments or community activity.
  • Mobile profile. Prefer the full social experience? Continue into Blindbandit Mobile after sign-in.
  • Privacy. Review the site privacy policy and community guidelines before posting.
  • Help. Email business@mrblindbandit.net for account assistance. Safety: safety@mrblindbandit.net.
