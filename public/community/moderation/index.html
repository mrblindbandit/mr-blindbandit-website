Care for the Blindbandit community

This moderation surface helps trusted operators review reports, hide harmful content, and escalate cases that need Social Admin controls on Blindbandit Mobile.

Access is restricted. Sign in with your production Clerk account that holds moderator privileges. Unprivileged visitors will see a denial state. Prefer documented actions with short rationale notes for the audit trail.

  • Triage. Sort new reports by severity: harm, impersonation, spam, rights claims.
  • Actions. Hide content, warn, restrict, or escalate to Social Admin for bans and device revocation.
  • Evidence. Capture links and timestamps; do not download illegal material to personal devices.
  • Appeals. Point users to moderation appeals and trust & safety pages when appropriate.
  • Cross-link. Use /mobile/admin/ for the full Social Admin Control Center.
