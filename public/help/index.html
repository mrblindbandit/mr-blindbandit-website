01

Listening and players

Open Music to browse releases or use the listening room. Player availability depends on the streaming service and your location or account. If a player will not load, use the official streaming link. Close the persistent player when you want playback to stop.

02

Account help

Fan accounts use community sign-in. Invited artists and staff use the separate label portal. Label invitations and password emails come from accounts@mrblindbandit.com. The sending domain differs from this website’s mrblindbandit.net address; account links lead back to the official website. For a label password, use the reset request page. If the email does not arrive, check spam and contact the label; do not post passwords or reset links publicly.

03

Send music or a business request

Use Submit music for demos and Professional inquiries for bookings, collaborations, licensing, or interviews. The submission form accepts a listening link or MP3/WAV file up to 10 MB. A saved request is not a promise of acceptance.

04

Accessibility and reporting problems

The site includes a skip link, labeled controls, keyboard navigation, and adjustable text, contrast, and motion preferences. Third-party players have their own interfaces. We do not claim an independent accessibility certification. If something blocks you, email business@mrblindbandit.net with the page address, device or assistive technology, and what you were trying to do. You can also request information in a different format.

05

Privacy, copyright, and community concerns

For privacy requests or copyright concerns, use business@mrblindbandit.net and the appropriate subject. Read the privacy policy, copyright policy, and community rules. Avoid sending unrelated sensitive information when reporting an issue.
