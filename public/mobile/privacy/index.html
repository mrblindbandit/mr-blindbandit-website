§03

Accounts and authentication

Blindbandit Mobile uses Clerk for sign-up and sign-in. Google and email/password (or email magic links, if enabled in Clerk) are supported. Apple Sign In is intentionally disabled for this product configuration unless later announced.

Clerk processes identifiers, email addresses, authentication events, device/browser signals, and fraud-prevention data under Clerk’s role as authentication provider. Anyone with a verified email through Clerk may create a Blindbandit Mobile profile and publish posts — label portal membership is not required.

The official verified artist profile for Mr. Blindbandit is linked to the Operator’s Clerk identity associated with kheckfinancial@gmail.com for ownership and verification integrity.

§04

Profile, posts, follows, and public visibility

Profiles may include handle, display name, bio, avatar, cover image, location, website, verification status, and monetization status. Posts, display names, handles, avatars, and verification badges are public by default when visibility is set to public.

Public content can be indexed, screenshotted, or copied by others. Do not post passwords, financial credentials, government IDs, home addresses of private individuals, intimate imagery, or another person’s personal data without authorization.

Follow relationships are used to build feeds, send notifications, and show social graph counts. Unfollow and account deletion/requests are available subject to legal retention duties.

§05

Messaging and calling

Direct messages are private between participants but are accessible to the Operator for abuse investigation, legal process, security, and integrity. Message content, timestamps, and participant identifiers are stored in the product database.

Voice and video calls use LiveKit. The Worker mints short-lived room tokens using LIVEKIT_API_KEY and LIVEKIT_API_SECRET held only as server secrets. The browser/app receives a token and room name — never the API secret. LiveKit and network providers may process IP addresses, connection metadata, and media transport information necessary to operate the call.

Call signaling events may trigger push notifications to the callee’s registered devices.

§06

Push notifications: Web Push, FCM, and APNs

Blindbandit Mobile supports (1) browser Web Push via the Push API and VAPID keys, (2) Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device tokens for iOS apps, and (3) Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens for Android apps.

When you enable notifications, we store encrypted or hashed device tokens / Web Push subscription endpoints, platform, app version, language, installation id, topic subscriptions, and delivery receipts needed to send and debug notifications. You can unregister devices and Web Push subscriptions from Settings or via API.

Notification categories include system/security, messages, calls, social activity, and optional marketing topics. Marketing topics require affirmative subscription where consent is required. You can manage topic subscriptions through /api/v1/push/topics endpoints.

Delivery providers (Apple, Google/FCM, browser push services) receive the minimum token and payload data needed to deliver the notification.

§07

Verification, monetization, and ads marketplace

Verification applications may include evidence links and statements you submit. Reviews are performed by the Operator. Approved verification sets a public verified badge.

Creator monetization applications may include payout email and program notes. Ads marketplace listings include title, body, destination URL, and budget metadata and remain subject to review before becoming active.

Financial payouts, if enabled later, will use additional providers disclosed at enrollment. Do not send bank passwords or full card numbers through free-text forms.

§09

Legal bases, retention, security, and rights

Processing relies on contract/requested service, legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, product integrity), legal obligations, and consent where required (optional marketing, certain cookies/ads).

Retention follows need: active account life, abuse and security windows, legal holds, and accounting duties. Backups may retain deleted data for a limited rolling period.

Security controls include HTTPS, auth checks on social routes, rate limits, audit logs for sensitive actions, encrypted device token storage via the platform vault when PLATFORM_VAULT_KEY is configured, and private vulnerability reporting at security@mrblindbandit.net.

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, and complaint rights. Email privacy@mrblindbandit.net. Philippine data subjects may contact the National Privacy Commission. EU/UK users may contact a competent supervisory authority where GDPR/UK GDPR applies to the processing.

§10

Children

Blindbandit Mobile is not directed to children under 13. Users under 13 may not create accounts, post, message, call, or enable push notifications. Older minors must comply with parental consent rules in their jurisdiction. See also the Child Safety Policy at /safety/ and Mobile Trust & Safety.

§11

International transfers and changes

Infrastructure may include Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, Clerk, LiveKit Cloud, Apple, Google, and other subprocessors. Transfers use appropriate contractual and technical safeguards available for the configured vendors.

Material changes will update the effective date. Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance where permitted by law. Prior versions may be requested from privacy@mrblindbandit.net.

§A

Subprocessors (illustrative)

Depending on configuration, Blindbandit Mobile may rely on: Cloudflare (Workers, D1, R2, DNS, CDN), Clerk (authentication), LiveKit Cloud (realtime media), Apple (APNs), Google (FCM / AdSense where enabled), Spotify (embeds), browser push services endpoint operators, and email providers such as Resend for transactional mail. §B

Data subject request procedure

Email privacy@mrblindbandit.net with “Privacy Request” in the subject. Describe the right you wish to exercise and the email/handle involved. We may need to verify identity (for example via the verified Clerk email on the account). We respond within the period required by applicable law, commonly within 30 days where GDPR-style timelines apply, subject to extensions and exceptions.

We will not delete information we must keep for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal claims, or law. Where deletion is limited, we will say so.

§C

Do Not Sell / Share and advertising choices

The Operator does not sell personal information for money. Advertising technology on eligible pages may constitute “sharing” or targeted advertising under some US state laws. Use Privacy Choices on the website, browser Global Privacy Control where honored by first-party controls, and Mobile notification topic opt-outs for marketing topics.

§D

API and app developers

Third-party or first-party apps calling /api/v1 must protect tokens, use TLS, avoid logging secrets, and present privacy disclosures to end users covering push tokens, call metadata, and message access. Developer abuse may result in credential revocation.
