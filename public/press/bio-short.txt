Mr. Blindbandit — short artist biography
Artist-supplied information

Mr. Blindbandit is Kaeleb Savon Heck, an American blind music creator and digital artist born in Salem, Virginia. Based in the Philippines, he works with accessible technology and screen readers to create music shaped by sound, memory and movement.
