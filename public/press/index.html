Artist biography

MR. BLINDBANDIT

Mr. Blindbandit is Kaeleb Heck, an American recording artist, producer, and content creator with Virginia roots and a home in the Philippines.

Blind since birth, he works with screen readers and accessible technology to create across ambient, cinematic, electronic, and hip-hop sounds. As founder of Blindbandit Records, he brings together independent music, video, and digital storytelling. His catalog includes Sapphire castles, Genesis, weightless, and cry forever, with creative connections including UUu Tang Maskman, Yxngfery, and Pablo Jackson Hairston.

Long biography

Identity and early life Mr. Blindbandit is the professional name of Kaeleb Savon Heck, an American blind music creator and digital artist born on April 4, 2000, in Salem, Virginia, in the Roanoke Valley. Heck was born completely blind with Leber Congenital Amaurosis (LCA), an inherited retinal disorder. He uses screen readers, audio feedback, a white cane, memory, touch and spatial awareness to navigate technology and physical environments.

Education During childhood, Heck lived in Virginia and Florida, including periods in Daytona Beach, Palm Coast and New Smyrna Beach. From 2012 until early 2016, he attended the Florida School for the Deaf and the Blind in St. Augustine, Florida, completing the eighth grade there before returning to Virginia. The school is historically associated with a number of notable blind and deaf alumni, including Ray Charles, who attended the institution as a child.

Music and creative practice Heck's creative work developed around sound as both an artistic medium and a practical way of understanding space. Working without visual waveforms, he uses accessible technology and screen readers to navigate music and digital production tools. His work under the Mr. Blindbandit name includes ambient, cinematic, electronic, experimental and story-driven material, with recurring themes of atmosphere, spatial sound, memory and movement.

Relationship and independent travel In November 2018, Heck met Emma, a woman from the Philippines, online. Their relationship became romantic on July 7, 2019 and continued long-distance for several years. On August 21, 2024, Heck traveled alone from the United States to the Philippines, using airport assistance, screen-reader technology and independent travel skills across multiple international connections. He later described the experience in the first-person story 'I Didn't See the Philippines for the First Time. I Heard It.'

Life in the Philippines After arriving in Davao, Heck met Emma in person for the first time. He later traveled to Cebu City to complete marriage-related requirements as a U.S. citizen. Heck and Emma were married in Kapalong, Philippines, on October 8, 2024, in a ceremony officiated by the mayor.

Public work Mr. Blindbandit's public identity combines music, accessibility, personal storytelling and independent digital creation. His official website, mrblindbandit.net, serves as a central hub for music, biography, videos, press information, stories, official links and contact information. The site's long-form story about his move to the Philippines should remain separate from this reference-style biography so visitors can choose between a concise factual overview and the first-person narrative.
