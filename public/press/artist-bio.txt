Mr. Blindbandit — Artist biography

Mr. Blindbandit is Kaeleb Heck, an American recording artist, producer, and content creator with Virginia roots and a home in the Philippines. Blind since birth, he creates with screen readers and accessible technology across ambient, cinematic, electronic, and hip-hop sounds. As founder of Blindbandit Records, he brings together independent music, video, and digital storytelling.
